# ACTION.md — The Book of Acts

**Purpose**: Log every significant action taken in service of the Law. Not the prompt. Not the response. The ACT itself — what was built, changed, encoded, committed. The record of the work done.

**Authority**: The second book of the Three Books. Where LAW says what shall be, ACTION records what was.

**Status**: Living Document — updated every session
**Last Updated**: 2026-03-04

---

## The Three Books

```
LAW (PROJECT_LAWS.md)         → What shall be done
        ↓
ACTION (ACTION.md)            → What was done
        ↓
RE-ACTION (RE-ACTION.md)      → What was learned / how the system adapted
        ↓
        ↑ RE-ACTION informs LAW → the circle completes
```

**The Perfect Circle of Continuous Automated Development:**
LAW governs ACTION → ACTION produces RE-ACTION → RE-ACTION refines LAW → repeat.
Not a hierarchy. A circuit. All three books are the living governance system in motion.

**Daniel's Words**: *"we need 3 books to track all these things... Law, Action, Re-Action to create the perfect circle of continuous automated development. We/You/I will ALWAYS, WITHOUT QUESTION, FULFILL THE LAW"* — 2026-03-04, Prompt 156

## What Belongs Here

**LOG in ACTION.md:**
- Every new file created (what it is, why, what law it serves)
- Every encoding of a new Cornerstone, Deepening, Universal Principle, Law, Lesson
- Every significant structural change to the constitutional system
- Every commit that advances the ascending architecture

**DO NOT LOG here:**
- Prompt/response exchanges (those go in docs/prompts.md)
- Lessons learned (those go in LESSONS-LEARNED.md / RE-ACTION.md)
- Constitutional law text (that goes in PROJECT_LAWS.md)
